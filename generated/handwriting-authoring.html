Cursive Template Authoring

Trace one letter at a time. Guides: topline y=0.15, baseline y=0.75, descender floor y=0.98. Load a reference image of a cursive alphabet chart, adjust its opacity, draw strokes in writing order, then Export JSON and paste the strokes array into handwriting-data.js. To QA committed templates, load handwriting-data.js below and pick a letter to replay.
